Dream Destination: Bikini Bottom

Located at the bottom of the Pacific Ocean beneath the Marshall Islands, Bikini Bottom is the ultimate underwater tropical paradise.
Home to vibrant sea life, iconic pineapple homes, and world-famous fast food, this 2-day trip promises non-stop aquatic adventures.

DAY 1

Meet, Greet, Downtown Exploration

  1. 8:00 AM - Breakfast at the Krusty Krab
    • Start the trip with Bikini Bottom's most iconic restaurant.
    • Try a Krabby Patty and enjoy the busy atmosphere.
  2. 10:00 AM - SpongeBob's Pineapple House
    • Visit SpongeBob's home.
    • Pet Gary the snail.
    • Take photos of the pineapple house.
  3. 12:00 PM - Patrick's Rock
    • Meet Patrict and try on his sand chair.
  4. 2:00 PM - Lunch at the Chum Bucket
    • Meet Plankton and his wife Karen.
    • Try their chum to see if it's really bad.
  5. 4:00 PM - Experiment at Sandy's Treedome
    • Visit Sandy Cheeks' famous underwater home.
    • Experience the dry environment inside the Treedome.
    • Try experimenting with acorn using Sandy's equipment
  6. 6:00 PM - Dinner at Goofy Goober's Ice Cream Party Boat
    • Try Triple Gooberberry Sunrise
    • Watch live performances by its mascot, Goofy Goober
  7. 8:00 PM - Ride the Flying Dutchman's Ship
    • Meet the Flying Dutchman, the legendary ghost pirate
    • Board the ghostly ship and explore its mysterious interior
  8. 10:00 PM - Go back to Hotel Halibut
    • Prepare stuffs to bring tomorrow for jellyfishing:
      • Jellyfish net
      • Glass Jar
      • Protective Glasses
    • Wash yourself and have a good rest

DAY 2

Jellyfishing, Goo Lagoon & Rock Bottom Adventure

  1. 8:00 AM - Breakfast at the Krusty Krab
    • Have one final breakfast at Bikini Bottom's most famous restaurant.
    • Meet SpongeBob and prepare for a day of exploration.
  2. 9:30 AM - Jellyfishing at Jellyfish Fields
    • Meet SpongeBob and Patrick for a morning of jellyfishing.
    • Catch jellyfish using a jellyfishing net.
    • Explore the famous Jellyfish Fields.
    • Take photos of the colorful jellyfish and underwater scenery.
  3. 12:00 PM - Picnic Lunch at Jellyfish Fields
    • Have lunch surrounded by the underwater scenery.
  4. 1:00 PM - Beach Activities at Goo Lagoon
    • Visit Bikini Bottom's famous underwater beach.
    • Swim and participate in beach activities.
  5. 3:00 PM - Adventure at Glove Universe
    • Visit the glove-themed amusement park.
    • Ride the Fiery Fist O' Pain II.
    • Try the Tunnel of Glove.
    • Explore the other attractions around the park.
  6. 5:00 PM - Journey to Rock Bottom
    • Travel toward the mysterious deep-sea city.
    • Experience the steep journey down into Rock Bottom.
  7. 5:30 PM - Explore Rock Bottom
    • Explore the dark streets of the deep-sea city.
    • Visit the Rock Bottom bus station.
    • Meet some of the strange deep-sea residents.
  8. 7:00 PM - Return to Bikini Bottom
    • Take the bus back from Rock Bottom.
  9. 8:00 PM - Farewell Dinner at the Krusty Krab
    • Enjoy one final Krabby Patty.
    • Say goodbye to SpongeBob, Patrick, and the Krusty Krab crew.
  10. 9:30 PM - Departure from Bikini Bottom
    • Collect your belongings and souvenirs.
